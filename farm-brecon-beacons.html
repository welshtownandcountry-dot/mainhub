Specialist Farm Pest Control in the Brecon Beacons

We deliver humane, poison-free Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for farms across the Brecon Beacons National Park and surrounding Powys areas.

Coverage includes: Brecon, Crickhowell, Talgarth, Sennybridge, Hay-on-Wye, Talybont-on-Usk, Llangorse, Defynnog, and beyond.

The rugged upland landscape — with iconic peaks like Pen y Fan (886m), Corn Du, Cribyn, and the Black Mountains edge — features moorland pastures, glaciated valleys, and river-fed farmlands along the Usk Valley.

These high-altitude habitats create unique pest challenges: moles in upland pastures, rabbits on moorland fringes, rodents in barns and outbuildings.

Led by RSPH Level 2 Certified Technician David Hill

We use prevention-first IPM:

  • Habitat modification
  • Exclusion barriers
  • Regular monitoring
  • Humane mechanical interventions only

No rodenticides — safe for livestock, working dogs, birds of prey, and sensitive National Park habitats (peat bogs, rivers, moorland).

Our methods are fully aligned with 2026 Red Tractor and SALSA standards — including audit-ready digital logs, site maps, photographic evidence, and risk assessments.

Common Pests We Control in Brecon Beacons Farms

Rodents (Rats & Mice)

Safe, non-toxic control in feed stores, barns, outbuildings, and valley-edge structures near the Usk River and A40 corridor.

Moles

Humane trapping to protect upland pastures, moorland soils, and grazing land from molehills and tunnelling around Pen y Fan and Black Mountains areas.

Rabbits

Perimeter proofing and humane exclusion to prevent burrowing, crop grazing, and land instability on open moorland and farm edges in the National Park.

Squirrels

Loft/building exclusion and humane removal for farm buildings and storage areas in rural holdings near historic market towns.
