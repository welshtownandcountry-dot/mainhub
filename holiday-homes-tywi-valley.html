Protect Your Tywi Valley Holiday Home

Valley Pest Experts provide humane, poison-free pest control for self-catering cottages and rural holiday lets across the Tywi Valley. We protect your property and your guests from rodents, squirrels, wasps, moles, and other common rural pests.
