Frequently Asked Questions

How long until the nest dies?

Most nests are fully inactive within 2–24 hours. Professional dusts/sprays are carried deep into the nest by worker wasps.

Do you offer pet-safe wasp removal?

Yes — we use targeted, low-risk treatments and can arrange pet sitting/walking through our partners at Julie’s Pet Services while we work. Your pets stay safe and stress-free.

Do you remove the dead nest?

Usually left in place (safe & prevents re-use). Physical removal possible in sensitive locations (e.g. solar equipment).

Are hornets worse than wasps?

Hornets sting harder but are less aggressive. We treat both species with equal care — active day & night.
