Protect Your Farm from Squirrels and Wildlife

Farms and rural properties across South & Mid Wales face constant pressure from wildlife such as squirrels, birds, and other small mammals. These animals can gnaw electrical cables, damage barns, contaminate feed, and compromise machinery. Humane & Effective Exclusion Methods

We prioritise non-lethal interventions whenever possible. This includes:

  • Installing stainless steel mesh and barriers on barn rafters, rooflines, and vents
  • Using high-tension humane traps for squirrels and small mammals
  • Monitoring wildlife movement patterns to prevent reinfestation
  • Removing access points and reinforcing weak structures
  • Combining wildlife exclusion with rodent control programs for maximum farm protection

Our approach ensures that your farm remains compliant with Red Tractor and SALSA audit requirements while keeping wildlife safe and the farm fully operational.
