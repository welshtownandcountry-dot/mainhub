Passed Audits Start with Professional Paperwork

By 2026, farm assurance schemes like **Red Tractor** and **SALSA** have significantly increased the requirement for professional Integrated Pest Management (IPM). Permanent toxic baiting is no longer the default; auditors now demand evidence of environmental risk assessments, proofing, and non-toxic monitoring before any chemical intervention.

At Welsh Town and Country Pest Control, we specialise in providing the professional digital audit trail that farmers need to protect their certification. Our poison-free approach isn't just better for your livestock; it's exactly what modern auditors want to see.

[Image of integrated pest management for farms]

What We Provide for Your Inspection:

  • Digital Visit Logs: Real-time reports delivered to your inbox with activity levels and action points.
  • Site Maps: Professional maps showing all numbered monitoring and trapping points.
  • IPM Evidence: Documented proof of habitat management and proofing to satisfy the "environmental hygiene" criteria.
  • RSPH Certification: Proof of qualified expertise (RSPH Level 2) as required by major schemes.
