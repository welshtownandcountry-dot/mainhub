Protect Your Holiday Home or Rural Let

Valley Pest Experts provide specialist, humane, and poison-free pest control for holiday homes, cottages, and rural lets in South & Mid Wales. Our services are designed to protect your property, your guests, and the surrounding environment while maintaining discreet, low-impact intervention.

Led by David Hill, RSPH Level 2 certified, we focus on long-term prevention through Integrated Pest Management (IPM), using monitoring, habitat modification, and proofing before any intervention. Where necessary, we use humane trapping and mechanical methods — no toxic chemicals required.

Our Holiday Home Pest Services

  • Rodent Control – Safe management of rats and mice in lofts, outbuildings, and guest areas.
  • Squirrel Exclusion – Proofing and removal from lofts, attics, and roof spaces.
  • Wasps & Hornets – Safe nest removal from eaves, gardens, and outdoor guest areas.
  • Mole Management – Lawn and garden protection, preventing burrows and unsightly damage.
  • Bird & Wildlife Proofing – Prevent nuisance birds from nesting and causing damage.
  • Pre-Season & Emergency Visits – Ensure properties are pest-free before guest arrival or respond quickly to any infestations.

We offer tailored plans for holiday homes, including seasonal checks, digital site reports, and minimal disruption for guests.
