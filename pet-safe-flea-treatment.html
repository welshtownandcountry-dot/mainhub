Coordinated Flea Eradication: The "Host & Home" Strategy

To successfully clear fleas from your property, you must understand the "95/5 Rule." Only 5% of an infestation is actually on your pet (the host); the remaining 95% is currently developing in your carpets, upholstery, and floorboards.

The Coordinated Treatment Day

I provide a specialized consultancy service that bridges the gap between pet care and pest management. For active infestations, I work in tandem with Julie’s Pet Services to ensure the infestation is hit from both sides simultaneously.

Phase 1: The Pet (The Host)

While I prepare the property, Julie’s Pet Services provides specialized flea shampoo treatments and professional bathing. This ensures that the animal is cleared of live breeders and eggs before they re-enter the home.

Phase 2: The Environment (The Home)

I manage the property using mechanical barriers and professional-grade applications. We focus on eliminating the "invisible" stages (eggs and pupae) that standard supermarket sprays cannot reach.

Why Standard Sprays Fail

Modern flea populations in South Wales have developed significant resistance to common supermarket chemicals. Furthermore, flea pupae are encased in a protective cocoon that is virtually impenetrable to poison.

Professional Field Tip: The Vibration Trigger

Flea pupae can lay dormant for months. They only hatch when they feel the vibration of a passing host. I advise all clients to vacuum daily after treatment; this vibration tricks the fleas into hatching, bringing them into contact with our professional-grade mechanical controls.
