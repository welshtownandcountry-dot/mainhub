Professional Flea Treatment & Pet-Safe Home Management Across South Wales

If you're dealing with fleas in your home, the problem isn't just on your pet. Up to 95% of an infestation lives within carpets, cracks and soft furnishings. Proper treatment must address both the animal and the environment at the same time.

Coordinated Host & Environment Control

Step 1 – Pet Management

Professional grooming and targeted flea cleansing ensures the animal is cleared while the property is treated.

Step 2 – Property Treatment

Mechanical control methods including professional-grade diatomaceous earth application and targeted heat management eliminate eggs and pupae hidden within the home.

Why Most Flea Treatments Fail

Shop-bought sprays often target only adult fleas. Eggs continue hatching for weeks. Without environmental management and daily vacuum stimulation, infestations return.

Field Experience Tip

Vacuuming triggers dormant flea pupae to hatch. This makes treatments effective instead of allowing the cycle to continue silently.
