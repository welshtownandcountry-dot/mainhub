How the "Total Clear" Partnership Works
A Coordinated Approach to Flea Eradication

Treating a flea infestation successfully requires more than just a bottle of spray. Fleas divide their time between the "Host" (your pet) and the "Environment" (your home). If you only treat one, the other will re-infest the property within days.

🤝 Our Exclusive Treatment Partnership

I have positioned Welsh Town & Country as a specialist consultant for flea management, partnering directly with Julie’s Pet Services to offer a synchronized solution. Here is how we break the cycle:

Phase 1: The Pet Spa (The Host)

Julie or Sara provides professional bathing and specialized flea shampoo treatments. While your home is being managed, your pet is safely cared for and cleaned, ensuring they don't bring live fleas back into the treated environment.

Phase 2: Environmental Barrier (The Home)

While the pet is with Julie, I provide the expertise and mechanical treatments needed for your home. We use pet-safe, non-toxic methods like specialized diatomaceous earth and heat to kill eggs and pupae hidden in carpets and skirting boards.

Why We Recommend Coordinated Treatment

Typical store-bought treatments fail because they don't address the 95% of the infestation that lives in your carpet as eggs and larvae. By coordinating with a professional groomer, we ensure that the minute your pet returns home, the house is a "hostile environment" for any remaining pests, and the pet is no longer a source of new eggs.

David’s Field Advice:

The "Vibration" Secret: Vacuuming isn't just for cleaning. The vibration of a vacuum cleaner mimics a host walking nearby, which triggers flea pupae to hatch. By vacuuming daily, you "trick" the fleas into hatching so our mechanical treatments can eliminate them.

Safe for Families & Paws

Because we focus on professional hygiene and mechanical barriers, our coordinated approach is 100% safe for households with young children, puppies, and senior pets. We avoid the heavy chemical "bombs" that often leave odors and residues behind.
