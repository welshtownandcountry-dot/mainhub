Complete Pet-Safe Flea Eradication

Eradicate fleas without exposing your family or pets to toxic pesticides. We use advanced, low-toxicity professional treatments designed specifically for homes with dogs, cats, and young children.

Why David Hill's Method is Different

Most "off-the-shelf" flea sprays contain harsh permethrins that can be harmful to cats. As a former Pestokil technician with 30+ years in the field, I use a poison-free approach where possible, focusing on Growth Regulators and physical dehydration agents that kill fleas but remain inert to mammals.

📋 Treatment Day Checklist

To get the best results, we recommend the following before I arrive:

  • Vacuum Everywhere: Especially under furniture and along skirting boards. Dispose of the bag immediately.
  • Hot Wash: Wash all pet bedding at 60°C.
  • Clear the Floors: Move toys and shoes so I can reach every inch of the carpet.
