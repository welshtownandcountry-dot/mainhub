David’s Expert Flea Prevention Tips

1. The 60°C Rule: Always wash pet bedding, rugs, and your own linens at 60°C. This is the only temperature guaranteed to kill flea eggs.

2. Vacuum Daily: During an outbreak, vacuum your carpets every single day. Focus on skirting boards and under the sofa—this "vibration" actually encourages flea pupae to hatch so they can be captured.

3. Treat the Pet, Not Just the Carpet: 95% of a flea infestation lives in the environment (your home), but the 5% on your pet are the breeders. Use Julie's professional bathing service to break the cycle.

Why Poison-Free?

Many modern flea populations in South Wales have developed resistance to common supermarket sprays. We recommend mechanical methods—like heat and specialized diatomaceous earth—which are 100% safe for kittens, puppies, and children's bedrooms.
