Treating a flea infestation properly requires more than just a spray. Fleas divide their life cycle between the host (your pet) and the environment (your home). If only one is treated, reinfestation can occur within days.

🤝 Coordinated Treatment Partnership

Welsh Town & Country works in coordination with trusted local grooming professionals to ensure both the pet and the property are addressed at the same time.

Phase 1: The Pet (Host Management)

Professional bathing and specialised flea treatments ensure your pet is cleaned and protected while your home is being treated.

Phase 2: The Property (Environmental Control)

I focus on mechanical and hygiene-based treatments including professional-grade diatomaceous earth application and targeted heat management to eliminate eggs and pupae in carpets, cracks and skirting boards.

Why Coordinated Flea Treatment Works

Up to 95% of a flea infestation lives within carpets and soft furnishings as eggs and larvae. By addressing both the pet and the environment simultaneously, we break the breeding cycle completely.

Field Advice from 30+ Years Experience

Vacuuming daily is essential. The vibration stimulates dormant pupae to hatch, allowing treatments to be effective. Without this step, infestations can linger for weeks.

Safe for Families & Pets

Our approach avoids heavy chemical foggers and harsh residues. Treatments are suitable for homes with children, puppies, elderly pets and sensitive households.
