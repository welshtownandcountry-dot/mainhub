Key Pest Risks Threatening Solar Farm Performance

Solar farms are engineered for decades of output — but pests can reduce efficiency, cause costly repairs, and create safety hazards if left unchecked.

Rodent Damage

Rats and mice chew through cables, wiring, junction boxes and inverters — leading to short circuits, fires, expensive downtime and insurance claims.

Rabbit Burrowing

Extensive rabbit warrens under panels cause soil subsidence, panel misalignment, water pooling and structural instability — particularly on rural greenfield sites.

Mole Tunnelling

Moles create networks of tunnels and molehills that undermine cabling trenches, foundation pads and ground-mount structures — risking long-term site stability.
