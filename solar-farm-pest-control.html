Specialist Solar Asset Protection

In the Tywi Valley, Ammanford, and across Carmarthenshire, solar farms face unique pest pressures. Unlike standard agricultural land, solar sites provide sheltered environments where rodents, rabbits, and moles thrive—posing a threat to your infrastructure.

At Welsh Town and Country Pest Control (Farm Division), we provide poison-free, audit-ready solutions that prevent costly O&M downtime and safeguard your renewable energy investment.

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice): Chewing cabling & wiring insulation, creating fire risks and system failures.
  • Rabbits: Burrowing that undermines mounting poles, causing subsidence and misalignment.
  • Moles: Tunneling that destabilizes soil structure around array foundations.
