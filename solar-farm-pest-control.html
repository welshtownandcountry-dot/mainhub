Valley Pest Experts delivers specialist non-toxic pest control for solar farms and PV arrays throughout South & Mid Wales — covering Tywi Valley, Carmarthenshire, Brecon Beacons, Powys, Ammanford, Llandeilo, Cross Hands, Neath/Resolven, and emerging sites like Llanwern, Heolddu near Ferryside, Brynrhyd near Llanedi, and others amid Wales' solar boom.

Led by RSPH Level 2 certified David Hill, we apply Integrated Pest Management (IPM) — focusing on prevention, monitoring, humane exclusion/trapping, and habitat management with **no poisons** to safeguard biodiversity, wildlife, and compliance on eco-enhanced solar sites. Sheltered panels create ideal conditions for pests: rabbits burrow causing subsidence/misalignment of mounts, rodents gnaw cables risking fires/electrical faults, moles tunnel destabilizing soil/foundations. Our discreet services include pre-install surveys, ongoing O&M monitoring, rapid response, and digital reports — minimizing downtime, protecting output, and supporting asset managers/insurers.
