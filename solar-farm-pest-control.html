Why Solar Farms in Wales Need Specialist Pest Protection

In the Tywi Valley, Gwendraeth Valley, Ammanford and Llandeilo areas, solar farms face increasing pest pressure from rodents (rats/mice), rabbits and moles. These animals cause:

  • Rodent damage: Chewing cables and wiring insulation, leading to electrical shorts, system failures and fire risks.
  • Rabbit burrowing: Undermining mounting poles, causing ground instability, subsidence and panel misalignment.
  • Mole activity: Tunnels under arrays destabilizing soil, risking structural issues and equipment damage.

These issues threaten O&M performance, insurance compliance and revenue from renewable energy sites across Carmarthenshire.

Common Pest Damage Risks to Solar PV Arrays

Pests exploit the warm, sheltered space under panels:

  • Rodents nest in junction boxes and chew soy-free insulation (modern wires still attractive).
  • Burrowing creates hot spots, reduces efficiency and increases fire risk from debris/nests.
  • Without protection, repairs cost thousands in downtime and fixes.
