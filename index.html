Frequently Asked Questions

1. What areas in Wales do you cover for pest control?
We provide comprehensive pest control services across South and Mid Wales, including Neath, Merthyr Tydfil, Brecon, Ammanford, Swansea, Cardiff, and surrounding valleys. Our coverage extends to towns like Resolven, Glynneath, Skewen, Aberfan, Rhymney, Llandeilo, and Crickhowell.
2. Are your rodent control methods poison-free?
Yes, we specialize in poison-free rodent control and are fully CRRU (Campaign for Responsible Rodenticide Use) compliant. We use humane traps, exclusion methods, and monitoring systems that eliminate risks to pets, wildlife, and the environment.
3. How much does wasp nest removal cost?
Our wasp nest removal service is priced at a fixed £80 per nest, with additional nests on the same property costing only £20 each. This includes complete removal and treatment to prevent recurrence.
4. Do you offer farm pest control for Red Tractor audits?
Absolutely. We provide audit-ready pest management specifically designed for Red Tractor 2026 compliance. Our services include rodent proofing, mole catching, documentation, and preventive measures that meet all certification requirements.
5. What's your experience with mole catching?
With over 30 years of experience, we specialize in traditional mole catching using proven methods. David Hill's expertise ensures effective mole removal while maintaining environmental balance on your property.
6. Are you available for emergency pest control?
Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency pest control services. Call us anytime at 07375 303124 for immediate assistance with urgent pest infestations.
7. What services do you provide for solar farms?
We protect solar assets with specialized rodent proofing for cable trays and inverter housings, perimeter monitoring systems, bird deterrent solutions, and 24/7 rapid response to prevent damage to your renewable energy infrastructure.
8. Are you qualified and insured?
Yes, we are fully insured, RSPH (Royal Society for Public Health) SUoR certified, and hold all necessary qualifications. David Hill has 30+ years experience including previous work with Pestokil.
9. Do you offer commercial pest control contracts?
Yes, we provide cost-effective commercial contracts for takeaways, shops, restaurants, and businesses. Our service includes discreet visits, detailed reporting for EHO inspections, and preventive maintenance.
10. How quickly can you respond to a pest problem?
We typically respond within 24 hours for standard appointments and offer same-day emergency service for urgent cases. Our rapid response team covers South and Mid Wales efficiently.
