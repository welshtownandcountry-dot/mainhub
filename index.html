What We Do & Where We Cover

At Welsh Town and Country Pest Control – Farm Division, we provide specialist, poison-free pest management tailored for the unique needs of Welsh farms, rural estates, and renewable energy sites. Led by David Hill, a RSPH Level 2 certified technician with years of practical experience, we focus exclusively on humane, environmentally responsible solutions that protect livestock, wildlife, crops, and infrastructure without relying on rodenticides or harmful chemicals.

Our core mission is to deliver long-term prevention and control through Integrated Pest Management (IPM) principles. This means we start with habitat modification, exclusion techniques, secure storage advice, and regular monitoring to reduce pest attractants before any intervention is needed. When action is required, we use traditional humane trapping, mechanical barriers, proofing, and targeted non-chemical methods. Every job is documented with digital records, site maps, risk assessments, and action logs — making us an ideal partner for farms preparing for the stricter 2026 Red Tractor and SALSA audit standards.

We specialise in the pests that cause the most headaches for Welsh agriculture and solar developments:

  • Rodents (rats & mice) – protecting feed stores, grain, buildings, and wiring from contamination, disease spread, and electrical damage.
  • Moles – preventing tunnel networks that destabilise soil, damage roots, and create hazards for machinery and livestock.
  • Rabbits – stopping burrowing that undermines fences, crop beds, and solar panel foundations, while reducing grazing damage.
  • Solar-specific threats – safeguarding PV arrays from rodent chewing on cables (fire risk), rabbit burrowing (subsidence), and mole activity (ground instability).

We also handle related rural pests such as squirrels in barns, pigeons in feed stores, and occasional bird or fox issues when they impact farm operations — always with humane, compliant methods.
