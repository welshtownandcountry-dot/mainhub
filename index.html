Poison-Free Pest Control for Rural Wales

Valley Pest Experts, trading as Welsh Town and Country Pest Control – Farm Division, provide specialist pest management for farms, solar arrays, and rural holiday properties across South & Mid Wales.

Led by David Hill, RSPH Level 2 certified, we focus on long-term prevention using Integrated Pest Management (IPM). Our approach prioritises habitat modification, monitoring, and proofing before intervention.

Where action is necessary, we use humane, poison-free methods — traps, exclusion, and mechanical solutions — backed by digital records and site reports to meet Red Tractor & SALSA 2026 audit standards.

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice) – Feed, grain, building & electrical protection
  • Moles – Prevent soil and pasture damage
  • Rabbits – Reduce burrowing damage to land and solar assets
  • Squirrels – Loft proofing & exclusion for holiday homes
  • Wasps & Hornets – Safe removal from eaves & gardens
  • Solar-specific risks – Cable protection, site safety, ground stability

Coverage: Ammanford, Llandeilo, Cross Hands, Carmarthen, Neath, Resolven, Tywi Valley, Brecon Beacons (Sennybridge, Talgarth, Crickhowell) and surrounding rural areas.
