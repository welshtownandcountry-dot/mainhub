Frequently Asked Questions

How much does wasp removal cost?
Treatment is a fixed £80 per nest. If there are additional nests on the same site, they are charged at £20 each.
Are your flea treatments safe for my pets?
Absolutely. While dogs cannot be present for 2 hours after the treatment is completed, our partnership with Julie’s Pet Services makes this easy to manage. We can arrange for your dog to be taken for a walk during this time, or even booked in for a professional flea bath and shampoo, returning them to a clean and ready for they safe home.
Do you offer 24/7 service?
Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency response for urgent infestations like rats, wasps, or fleas across South and Mid Wales.
Are you insured and certified?
Yes. We are RSPH SUoR Certified, fully insured, and David Hill brings over 30 years of field experience, including time as a Pestokil technician.
