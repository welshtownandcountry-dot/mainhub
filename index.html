What We Do & Where We Cover

At Welsh Town and Country Pest Control – Farm Division, we provide specialist, poison-free pest management tailored for the unique needs of Welsh farms, rural estates, and renewable energy sites. Led by David Hill, a RSPH Level 2 certified technician, we focus exclusively on humane, environmentally responsible solutions that protect livestock, wildlife, and infrastructure.

Our core mission is to deliver long-term prevention through **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**. We prioritise habitat modification and physical exclusion before intervention. When action is required, we use traditional humane trapping and mechanical barriers. Every job is backed by digital records — ideal for farms preparing for **2026 Red Tractor and SALSA audit standards**.

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice) – Protecting feed, grain, and wiring from contamination and fire risks.
  • Moles – Managed using traditional methods to prevent soil instability and root damage.
  • Rabbits – Stopping burrowing that undermines foundations and solar array stability.
  • Solar-specific threats – Safeguarding PV arrays from cable damage and ground subsidence.

Where we cover: We serve the entirety of South and Mid Wales. Our main service footprint includes Ammanford, Llandeilo, Cross Hands, Carmarthen, Neath, Resolven, and the Brecon Beacons (including Sennybridge, Talgarth, and Crickhowell).
