Frequently Asked Questions

1. What areas in Wales do you cover?
South and Mid Wales including Neath, Merthyr Tydfil, Brecon, Ammanford, Swansea, Cardiff and surrounding valleys/towns (Resolven, Glynneath, Skewen, Aberfan, Rhymney, Llandeilo, Crickhowell etc.).
2. Are your rodent control methods poison-free?
Yes — we specialise in poison-free rodent control, fully CRRU compliant, using humane traps, exclusion and monitoring to protect pets, wildlife and the environment.
3. How much does wasp nest removal cost?
Fixed £80 per nest, £20 for each additional nest on the same property. Includes full treatment and prevention advice. Pet-safe coordination available.
4. Do you offer farm pest control for Red Tractor audits?
Yes — audit-ready pest management designed for Red Tractor 2026 compliance, including proofing, mole catching and full documentation.
5. What's your experience with mole catching?
Over 30 years, specialising in traditional mole catching with proven methods. Effective removal while maintaining environmental balance.
6. Are you available for emergency pest control?
Yes — 24/7 emergency service. Call 07375 303124 anytime for urgent infestations (rats, wasps, fleas etc.).
7. Do you offer pet-safe flea & wasp treatments?
Yes — all flea and wasp treatments are pet-safe and poison-free where possible. We partner with Julie’s Pet Services (Neath/Swansea) so your pets can be walked, bathed or cared for during treatment and returned to a safe home.
