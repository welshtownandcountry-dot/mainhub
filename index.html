Our Core Specialisms

Poison-Free Farm Pest Management in Tywi Valley

Agricultural pest control is undergoing a revolution. With increasing secondary poisoning risks to owls and birds of prey, many farmers in Carmarthenshire and the Tywi Valley are seeking alternatives to traditional baiting. At Valley Pest Experts, we implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM).

By focusing on "Proofing and Exclusion," we identify how rodents enter your feed stores or barns and physically block them. This approach is not only more humane but significantly more effective for Red Tractor and SALSA audits. Our 2026 digital reporting ensures you have a full paper trail for every inspection, showing a proactive, non-toxic approach that modern auditors love.

Solar Farm Protection: Safeguarding Wales' Green Energy

Solar arrays are unique environments that attract specific pest risks. Rabbits and moles can undermine mounting structures, while rodents view expensive cabling as a perfect chewing material. A single rodent incursion can lead to thousands of pounds in electrical damage and site downtime.

We provide specialised Solar Farm Pest Control across South Wales, focusing on perimeter integrity and cable protection. Our technicians are trained to work around sensitive PV equipment, ensuring your renewable energy investment remains productive and pest-free without introducing harmful toxins into the local ecosystem.

Holiday Home & Rural Let Compliance: Brecon Beacons

For owners of holiday cottages in the Brecon Beacons, a pest sighting can be a business disaster. Guests expect a pristine, rural experience, not unwanted visitors in the attic. We offer a "Discrete Protection" service for rural lets.

We go beyond simple trapping; we perform high-level Squirrel and Wildlife Exclusion. This involves proofing eaves, sealing entry points with stainless steel mesh, and ensuring that your property is impenetrable. Because we use poison-free methods, there is zero risk to guests’ pets or local wildlife—a major selling point for eco-conscious holiday home owners.
