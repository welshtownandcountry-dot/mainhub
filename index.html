Professional Pest Control for Farms, Solar Sites & Commercial Premises

Valley Pest Experts provides specialist pest control services for farms, solar farms, industrial sites, commercial properties and rural homes across South and Mid Wales.

All work is carried out using poison-free, targeted control methods wherever possible, with a strong focus on compliance, record-keeping and long-term prevention rather than short-term fixes.

Operated by David Hill, an experienced pest control technician with an ex-Pestokil background, the service is designed for landowners, farmers and businesses that need results they can stand behind during inspections.
