The 2026 IPM Standard

At Valley Pest Experts, we implement Integrated Pest Management (IPM). By focusing on "Proofing and Exclusion," we physically block pests from entering your feed stores or solar arrays.

This proactive, non-toxic approach is preferred by modern auditors. Our digital reporting ensures you have a full paper trail for every inspection. We protect the land for future generations by avoiding blanket baiting that harms the Red Kite and Barn Owl.
