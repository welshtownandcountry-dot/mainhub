The 2026 Standard: Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

At Welsh Town and Country Pest Services, we have moved beyond the outdated "box-and-bait" model. In 2026, the Environmental Agency and CRRU (Campaign for Responsible Rodenticide Use) have tightened regulations surrounding the use of chemical poisons in the Welsh countryside. Our approach is built on the IPM Pyramid, ensuring that lethal chemical intervention is the last resort, not the first.

The IPM Hierarchy for Welsh Agriculture

Tier 1: Physical Proofing - Blocking entry points with steel mesh and rapid-set compounds.

Tier 2: Sanitation - Removing the food sources that attract rats and mice to your feed stores.

Tier 3: Non-Toxic Monitoring - Using UV-reactive tracking and digital sensors.

Tier 4: Mechanical Control - Precision trapping (poison-free) to clear active infestations.

Why Poison-Free is the Future of Wales

The reliance on second-generation anticoagulant rodenticides (SGARs) has led to widespread secondary poisoning of Welsh birds of prey, specifically the Red Kite and Barn Owl populations in the Tywi Valley and Brecon Beacons. By choosing Welsh Town and Country Pest Services, you are opting for a "Proofing First" methodology. We identify how the pests are entering—be it via defective drainage or structural gaps—and we fix the building, not just the pest.

The "River Rat" Challenge in the Welsh Valleys

Towns like Neath, Merthyr, and Ammanford often face unique challenges with Rattus norvegicus (the Brown Rat) due to the proximity of river systems and aging Victorian drainage networks. These "River Rats" are highly mobile and often enter terraced houses via shared drainage stacks.

CCTV Drain Surveys & Rat Valves

If you have a recurring rat problem in your kitchen or bathroom, the issue is almost certainly a break in the sewer pipe. As part of Welsh Town and Country Pest Services, we utilize CCTV drain inspection cameras to locate these defects. Once found, we install stainless steel rat-blocker valves. These valves allow waste to flow out but prevent rats from swimming up into your property. This is a permanent, one-time fix that eliminates the need for endless baiting.

Traditional Mole Catching: "No Catch, No Fee"
