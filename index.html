What We Do & Where We Cover

Valley Pest Experts, trading as Welsh Town and Country Pest Control – Farm Division, provide specialist, poison-free pest management for Welsh farms, rural estates and renewable energy sites.

Led by David Hill, an RSPH Level 2 certified technician, our work focuses on long-term prevention through Integrated Pest Management (IPM). We prioritise habitat modification, proofing and monitoring before intervention.

Where action is required, we use humane trapping and mechanical control methods only — supported by digital records and site reports designed to meet 2026 Red Tractor and SALSA audit standards.

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice) – Protecting feed, grain and electrical systems
  • Moles – Preventing soil damage and livestock injury
  • Rabbits – Reducing burrowing damage to land and solar assets
  • Solar-specific risks – Cable protection and ground stability

Coverage: South & Mid Wales including Ammanford, Llandeilo, Cross Hands, Carmarthen, Neath, Resolven and the Brecon Beacons (Sennybridge, Talgarth & Crickhowell).
