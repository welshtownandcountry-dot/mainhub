Why Choose Exclusion Over Reactive Control?

Reactive trapping addresses symptoms, not the root cause. Proofing is a one-time investment offering years of protection. Specialist mesh, spikes, and barriers make the site inaccessible, safe, and 100% poison-free, suitable for organic farms, wildlife-sensitive areas, and locations near watercourses — perfect for barn bird proofing Red Tractor audits or solar array bird mesh Ammanford installations.
