The Importance of Physical Proofing in 2026

As we move into 2026, the landscape of pest control in Wales has shifted significantly. With stricter regulations on rodenticides and a growing emphasis on biodiversity, physical proofing (also known as pest exclusion) has become the gold standard for protecting rural assets. At Welsh Town and Country Pest Control – Farm Division, led by David Hill, we believe that the best way to manage a pest problem is to ensure it never starts.

Birds and squirrels are often underestimated until the damage is done. In an agricultural setting, birds can contaminate massive quantities of livestock feed, while squirrels can compromise the structural integrity of historical barns and modern outbuildings. In the renewable energy sector, these same pests pose a direct threat to the sensitive wiring and stability of solar PV arrays. Our service focuses on identifying the vulnerabilities in your infrastructure and sealing them with high-grade, industrial-strength materials.

Why Choose Exclusion Over Reactive Control?

Traditional pest control often relies on a cycle of trapping or removal. While necessary during an active infestation, it doesn't address the root cause. Proofing is a one-time investment that provides years of protection. By installing specialist mesh, spikes, and structural barriers, we alter the habitat to make it unattractive or inaccessible to target pests. This method is 100% poison-free, making it safe for organic farms, wildlife-sensitive areas, and sites near watercourses.

Our Specialised Proofing Solutions

1. Solar Panel Bird Proofing (Pigeon Exclusion)

Solar farms across the Tywi Valley and Carmarthenshire are increasingly plagued by pigeons. The gap between the panels and the roof or ground provides a perfect, sheltered nesting site. However, the presence of birds leads to a build-up of guano (droppings), which is highly corrosive and can damage the panel frames. Furthermore, the debris from nesting materials increases the risk of "hotspots" and fire hazards.

We use specialist solar-grade mesh and clips that do not require drilling into the panel frames, preserving your manufacturer's warranty. This creates a permanent barrier that prevents birds from getting underneath the array, forcing them to move elsewhere without harming them.

2. Squirrel Exclusion for Barns & Outbuildings

Grey squirrels are a destructive force in Welsh rural properties. Their need to gnaw means that electrical wiring, lead flashing, and timber beams are all at risk. A single squirrel family in a roof void can cause thousands of pounds in damage in just one season.

Our proofing process involves a comprehensive "top-to-bottom" survey. We identify entry points as small as a golf ball and seal them using heavy-gauge galvanised steel mesh or structural repairs. We also provide advice on tree canopy management to prevent squirrels from jumping onto your roof in the first place.

3. Bird Spiking & Netting for Feed Stores

For farms preparing for Red Tractor or SALSA audits, bird contamination in feed stores is a major non-conformance risk. Pigeons and starlings carry various pathogens that can impact livestock health. We install professional netting systems and bird spikes to keep birds away from rafters and loading bays, ensuring your grain and silage remain clean and compliant.

Frequently Asked Proofing Questions

Is proofing more expensive than trapping?
Initially, proofing has a higher upfront cost because of the materials and labor involved. However, because it prevents future infestations, it is significantly cheaper than paying for recurring call-outs and pest removals over several years.

Can you proof historical farm buildings?
Yes. We use sympathetic proofing methods that protect the structure without ruining its aesthetic. We use stainless steel or copper meshes that blend into the shadows of eaves and rafters.

Will bird mesh affect my solar panel efficiency?
Not at all. The mesh is installed around the perimeter of the array to block access underneath. It does not cover the surface of the panels and does not interfere with the electrical output.
