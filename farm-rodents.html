Why Rodent Control is Critical on Farms

Rodents can rapidly multiply and cause devastating damage. Rats and mice contaminate feed, gnaw electrical wiring, spread disease, and compromise livestock welfare. Left unmanaged, infestations can lead to expensive losses, disrupt farm operations, and create compliance issues for Red Tractor & SALSA audits.

Humane, preventive strategies are essential. This includes rodent monitoring, exclusion work in barns and storage areas, and ongoing farm inspections. Our approach ensures that your farm is not only protected but also documented with audit-ready digital logs for peace of mind.

Humane Rodent Solutions for Long-Term Farm Protection

At Valley Pest Experts, our primary goal is to provide long-term rodent control for farms in Wales without relying on toxic chemicals. Using Integrated Pest Management (IPM), we focus on prevention, monitoring, habitat management, and humane mechanical interventions. This approach reduces reliance on poison and ensures compliance with Red Tractor and SALSA 2026 standards.

Our team inspects barns, feed stores, silos, and livestock areas to detect signs of rat and mice activity. We install humane traps, rodent-proofing measures, and exclusion systems to prevent further damage. By documenting all activity in digital rodent control logs, we provide evidence for audits and maintain complete transparency. This makes our service ideal for farms needing robust, non-toxic rodent management while also addressing other pests such as moles and rabbits.

Whether it’s a small farm in the Tywi Valley or a large dairy in the Brecon Beacons, our solutions are tailored to each site’s requirements.
