Modern IPM for Welsh Agriculture

We specialise in **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)** — a proactive, sustainable approach that prioritises prevention, regular monitoring, and non-chemical methods. This aligns with the **2026 Red Tractor** emphasis on streamlined audits and evidence-based records.

Common pests on farms in Carmarthenshire, Tywi Valley, and the Brecon Beacons include rodents, moles, and rabbits. Our poison-free methods ensure total safety for your livestock, working dogs, and the surrounding environment.
