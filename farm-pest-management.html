Poison-Free Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for Welsh Farms

We deliver sustainable, proactive Integrated Pest Management (IPM) — prioritising prevention, monitoring, habitat modification, exclusion, and humane trapping with no poisons. Fully aligned with 2026 Red Tractor & SALSA standards (requiring competent non-toxic approaches), our services protect farms across South & Mid Wales, including Carmarthenshire Tywi Valley, Brecon Beacons, Powys, and areas near River Tywi, Black Mountains, and Tywi Valley Path.

Key pests: rodents in barns/feed stores (contamination/disease risks), moles in pastures (molehills disrupting grazing/machinery), rabbits (burrowing damaging crops/land). We provide humane mole trapping Brecon Beacons farms, rabbit burrowing prevention Powys agricultural land, non-toxic rodent control farms Carmarthenshire, plus audit-ready digital logs, site surveys, and compliance documentation for farm pest audit Llandovery or Red Tractor 2026 pest compliance support.
