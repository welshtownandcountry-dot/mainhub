Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for Welsh Farms

At Valley Pest Experts, we follow **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)** principles: long-term prevention through monitoring, habitat management, and non-chemical methods first, minimising risks to people, livestock, wildlife and the environment.

Common farm pests in South & Mid Wales (Carmarthenshire, Tywi Valley, Brecon Beacons) include rodents (rats/mice), moles and rabbits, which damage crops, stored feed, buildings, and infrastructure while spreading disease and risking Red Tractor/SALSA audit failures.

Key Farm Pest Problems & Risks

  • Rodents: Contaminate feed, chew wiring/cables, spread diseases (e.g. leptospirosis, salmonella), attract secondary pests.
  • Moles: Create tunnels leading to subsidence, uneven ground, damaged machinery/roots.
  • Rabbits: Burrow under fences/buildings, eat young crops/plants, undermine structures.

Without proper management, these issues lead to financial losses, compliance failures and animal health risks.

Our Poison-Free IPM Approach

  • Prevention First: Site proofing (sealing entry points, vegetation control), hygiene (spill cleanup, secure feed storage), habitat modification to reduce harbourage.
  • Regular Monitoring: Weekly/quarterly farm surveys for signs (droppings, runs, burrows) using non-toxic indicators to detect activity early.
  • Non-Chemical Controls: Humane trapping (spring/live-catch), mechanical exclusion (mesh, fencing), natural predators encouragement where safe.
  • Targeted Intervention: Only when needed – low-risk, poison-free methods with digital records for traceability.
  • Red Tractor & SALSA Compliance: Full audit-ready documentation (site plans, risk assessments, action logs) aligned with 2025/2026 standards – streamlined records, no unnecessary bureaucracy.
