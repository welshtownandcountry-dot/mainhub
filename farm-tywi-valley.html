Specialist Farm Pest Control in the Tywi Valley

We provide humane, poison-free Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for farms throughout the Tywi Valley and wider Carmarthenshire.

Coverage includes: Llandeilo, Carmarthen, Cross Hands, Ffairfach, Nantgaredig, Llanarthne, Capel Isaac, Pontyberem, Pontyates, Tumble, Trimsaran and surrounding areas.

The fertile, river-fed landscape along the River Tywi (Afon Tywi/Towy) supports some of Wales' most productive lowland agriculture — lush pastures, silage fields, mixed woodlands, gentle slopes, and historic sites like Dinefwr Castle, Dryslwyn Castle, Carreg Cennen Castle, the National Botanic Garden of Wales, and Aberglasney Gardens.

But the river corridors, damp soils, and biodiversity hotspots create specific pest pressures that demand sustainable, non-toxic solutions.

Led by RSPH Level 2 Certified Technician David Hill

We deliver prevention-first IPM:

  • Habitat modification
  • Exclusion barriers
  • Regular monitoring
  • Humane mechanical interventions only

No rodenticides — ever. This keeps livestock, wildlife (otters, kingfishers, birds of prey), pollinators, and the River Tywi ecosystem safe.

Our methods are fully aligned with 2026 Red Tractor and SALSA standards — including audit-ready digital logs, site maps, photographic evidence, and risk assessments.

Common Pests We Control in Tywi Valley Farms

Rodents (Rats & Mice)

Non-toxic control in feed stores, grain barns, milking parlours, outbuildings, and river-adjacent structures along the Tywi and A40 corridor.

They cause feed contamination, disease spread (Leptospirosis), and structural damage in damp, food-rich environments.

Moles

Humane trapping to protect pastures, silage fields, and soil structure from molehills, tunnelling, and root disturbance in fertile lowland areas around Llandeilo, Carmarthen, Ffairfach, and Cross Hands.

Rabbits

Perimeter proofing and humane exclusion to prevent burrowing, crop grazing, and land instability on valley slopes and fertile farmland near the River Tywi.

Squirrels

Loft/building exclusion and humane removal for farm outbuildings and storage areas near historic sites like Dinefwr Castle and Aberglasney Gardens.
