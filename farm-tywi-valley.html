Farm Pest Control in Tywi Valley

We specialise in humane, poison-free pest management for farms across the Tywi Valley and surrounding Carmarthenshire areas (Llandeilo, Carmarthen, Cross Hands, Ffairfach, Nantgaredig, Llanarthne, Capel Isaac, and beyond). The fertile river valley along the River Tywi (Towy), with its lush pastures, silage fields, mixed woodlands, and gentle slopes, is one of Wales' most productive agricultural regions. Proximity to historic landmarks like Dinefwr Castle, Dryslwyn Castle, Carreg Cennen Castle, the National Botanic Garden of Wales, and Aberglasney Gardens adds to its appeal, but the river-adjacent habitats, lowland/upland transitions, and rich biodiversity create unique pest pressures that require careful, sustainable management.

Led by RSPH Level 2 certified technician David Hill, Valley Pest Experts deliver **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)** solutions tailored to Tywi Valley farms. IPM emphasises prevention through habitat modification, exclusion barriers, regular monitoring, and cultural practices — using humane trapping and mechanical methods only when necessary, never relying on rodenticides that could harm livestock, wildlife, or the River Tywi ecosystem. This approach aligns perfectly with modern farming standards and helps maintain soil health, biodiversity, and long-term productivity in this scenic, river-fed landscape.

Pests We Handle in Tywi Valley Farms

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice) – Safe, non-toxic control in feed stores, grain barns, outbuildings, and river-adjacent structures common along the River Tywi and A40 corridor. Rats and mice thrive in damp, food-rich environments, causing contamination, structural damage, and disease risks to livestock and stored crops.
  • Moles – Protect pastures, silage fields, and soil structure from molehills, tunneling, and root disturbance in the fertile lowland areas around Llandeilo, Carmarthen, and Ffairfach. Mole activity disrupts grazing, machinery, and field drainage — we use humane trapping to restore land without chemicals.
  • Rabbits – Minimise burrowing, crop grazing, and land instability on fertile Tywi farmland and valley slopes. Rabbits damage young crops, silage, and pasture quality, leading to economic losses; our proofing and humane exclusion methods protect yields sustainably.
  • Squirrels – Humane exclusion and loft proofing for farm buildings, storage areas, and outbuildings in rural holdings near historic sites like Dinefwr Castle. Grey squirrels cause wiring damage, insulation contamination, and noise issues in barns and lofts.
