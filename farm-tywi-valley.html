Farm Pest Control in Tywi Valley

We specialise in humane, poison-free pest management for farms across the Tywi Valley and surrounding Carmarthenshire areas (Llandeilo, Carmarthen, Cross Hands, Ffairfach, Nantgaredig, Llanarthne, Capel Isaac, etc.). The fertile river valley along the River Tywi (Towy), with its pastures, silage fields, and proximity to landmarks like Dinefwr Castle, Carreg Cennen Castle, Dryslwyn Castle, and the nearby National Botanic Garden of Wales, faces unique pest pressures from river-adjacent habitats and lowland/upland transitions. Led by RSPH Level 2 certified David Hill, we use Integrated Pest Management (IPM) — focusing on prevention, monitoring, habitat modification, and humane trapping rather than rodenticides.

Pests We Handle in Tywi Valley Farms

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice) – Safe, non-toxic control in feed stores, grain barns, outbuildings and river-adjacent structures common in the valley near the River Tywi and A40 corridor.
  • Moles – Protect pastures, silage fields and soil structure from molehills and tunneling damage in the fertile lowland areas around Llandeilo and Carmarthen.
  • Rabbits – Minimise burrowing, crop grazing and land instability on fertile Tywi farmland and valley slopes.
  • Squirrels – Humane exclusion and loft proofing for farm buildings and storage areas in rural holdings near historic sites like Dinefwr Castle.
