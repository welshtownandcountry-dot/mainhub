Specialist Farm Pest Control in the Tywi Valley

We provide humane, poison-free Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for farms throughout the Tywi Valley and wider Carmarthenshire (Llandeilo, Carmarthen, Cross Hands, Ffairfach, Nantgaredig, Llanarthne, Capel Isaac, Pontyberem, Pontyates, Tumble, Trimsaran, and surrounding areas). The fertile, river-fed landscape along the River Tywi (Afon Tywi/Towy) – with lush pastures, silage fields, mixed woodlands, gentle slopes, and historic sites like Dinefwr Castle, Dryslwyn Castle, Carreg Cennen Castle, the National Botanic Garden of Wales, and Aberglasney Gardens – supports some of Wales' most productive lowland agriculture. However, the river corridors, damp soils, and biodiversity hotspots create specific pest pressures that demand sustainable, non-toxic solutions.

Led by RSPH Level 2 certified technician David Hill, Valley Pest Experts delivers prevention-first IPM: habitat modification, exclusion barriers, regular monitoring, and humane mechanical interventions only – never rodenticides that could harm livestock, wildlife (otters, kingfishers, birds of prey), pollinators, or the River Tywi ecosystem. Our methods are fully aligned with 2026 Red Tractor and SALSA standards, providing audit-ready digital logs, site maps, photographic evidence, and risk assessments to keep your farm compliant and productive.

Common Pests We Control in Tywi Valley Farms

  • Rodents (Rats & Mice) – Non-toxic control in feed stores, grain barns, milking parlours, outbuildings, and river-adjacent structures along the Tywi and A40 corridor. Rodents cause feed contamination, disease spread (Leptospirosis), and structural damage in damp, food-rich environments.
  • Moles – Humane trapping to protect pastures, silage fields, and soil structure from molehills, tunnelling, and root disturbance in fertile lowland areas around Llandeilo, Carmarthen, Ffairfach, and Cross Hands.
  • Rabbits – Perimeter proofing and humane exclusion to prevent burrowing, crop grazing, and land instability on valley slopes and fertile farmland near the River Tywi.
  • Squirrels – Loft/building exclusion and humane removal for farm outbuildings and storage areas near historic sites like Dinefwr Castle and Aberglasney Gardens.
